# Apps to disable on a TCL Google TV with the G10 board (tested firmware STT2.230203.001, product G10_4K_GB_NF_32BIT).
# Adapted from the community list in https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv (examples/tcl-g10-google-tv, MIT licence).
# Disabled, never uninstalled: tools/debloat.sh <ip> --undo turns them back on.
# Live TV (com.tcl.tv), inputs and the source panel are protected by the script and are never touched.

com.tcl.interactive                    # TCL background service
com.tcl.exhibit                        # store demo mode
com.tcl.esticker                       # store demo stickers
