Your own art for the big left screen
====================================
Put a PNG here named after the app's package and it is shown on the left screen
when that app is focused, instead of the app banner or icon.

  hero/com.netflix.ninja.png
  hero/com.google.android.youtube.tv.png

Tips
- Portrait art around 440 x 540 px fills the slot; larger images are scaled to cover it.
- The slot has rounded corners (about 46 px), so keep important parts away from the edges.
- Use art you are allowed to use. Copyrighted characters are fine for a private device
  at home, but do not publish them in a public repository or release.
- Without a file here the launcher falls back to the app's own TV banner, then to its icon.
